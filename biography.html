Jesse Potter
Home Biography Community Social Contact

Centered in Wolfville, Nova Scotia, Jesse Potter has cultivated a musical career spanning nearly two decades. He began performing his original music at fourteen, shaped by the vitality and support of the Annapolis Valley music community.

Jesse went on to lead and contribute to projects including The Funtime Brigade, WineHardt: Django Uncorked, and his long-running folk-rock group The Chimney Swifts. In 2016, his song “Travel The World” carried the band into the regional Top 10 of CBC Searchlight.

Today, Jesse’s warm performances pair evocative lyrics with expressive guitar, blending blues and jazz with soulful folk storytelling. His songs invite audiences to connect, reflect, and leave with a renewed sense of wonder.

Another early project and another step toward a broader, more adventurous musical identity.

Jesse releases his first solo album and establishes an early recorded catalogue of original songs.

2011

The Chimney Swifts begin

A project that would grow through many forms and become one of the longest-running threads of Jesse's career.

2012–2014

The Funtime Brigade

A playful “Hip-Rock Folk-Hop” era that expanded Jesse's writing into rap and produced the live album Just A Whittle Bit.

2013

Cross-Canada tour

Touring with Micah O'Connell takes Jesse through Ontario, Alberta, and British Columbia.

2013-2015

WineHardt

Gypsy-jazz ensemble work adds another musical language to an increasingly wide-ranging body of experience.

2016

CBC Searchlight Regional Top 10

“Travel The World,” written by Jesse, carries The Chimney Swifts into the regional Top 10 of CBC Searchlight.

2017 & 2018

Eastern Canada tours

Two more tours across Eastern Canada expand Jesse's live experience and regional reach.

2017–2018

Dooly's Open Mic

Hosting the New Minas open mic deepens Jesse's role as a facilitator and connector within the local scene.

2019

Astronomy

The Chimney Swifts release an album recorded and produced by Jesse, alongside festival and theatre performances.
